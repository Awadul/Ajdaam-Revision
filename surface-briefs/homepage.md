# Homepage continuation brief

## Status and authority

Mode: Persuade. Direction: Two Equal Worlds. The user approved the earlier mood board's aesthetic and explicitly chose equal division prominence. The latest homepage image illustrates the developed composition; it was displayed but has not received a separate explicit approval as a pixel-exact build contract. It is a design reference, not a tested interface.

## First viewport

One shared ivory header with the original logo and visible links to Engineering & Manufacturing, Bath & Sanitary, About and Enquire. Proposed headline from the concept: 'Precision in making. Refinement in living.' Proposed descriptor: 'Engineering, manufacturing and sanitary products.' These are proposed copy, not independently verified performance claims.

Two equal-width, equal-height photographic destinations on desktop. Matching caption area, heading hierarchy and action prominence. Engineering action: Explore capabilities. Sanitary action: Explore products. No hover expansion that reduces the other division. Do not make one panel's photographic subject overwhelming relative to the other.

## Responsive behaviour

On mobile show both division links before large photography. Stack the imagery while maintaining equal role styling. Preserve a usable enquiry action. Test wrapping, keyboard navigation, 200% zoom and small screens; image dimensions are not responsive specifications.

## Page sequence

Shared introduction -> paired division destinations -> balanced engineering work and sanitary collection previews -> relevant evidence for each -> company context -> appropriate enquiry routes. Avoid a mandatory entry gate, long cinematic preamble or news before useful offerings.

## Evidence and provenance

Engineering: verified processes, materials, capacities, inspection and relevant work. Sanitary: actual categories, manufacturer, dimensions, finish, installation information and verified product status. Use 'Manufactured by Ajdaam' or 'Supplied by Ajdaam' only after item-level confirmation. Do not imply every supplied fitting passes through Ajdaam's workshop.

## Motion starting points

Proposed, untested: control feedback 120-180 ms; single heading entrance 450-600 ms; occasional image reveal 500-700 ms; fixed-position finish image change 180-250 ms; menu open/close 250/180 ms. No essential information hidden pending animation. Reduced-motion mode removes nonessential transforms and reveals. Real footage needs playback controls where applicable and a useful still fallback.

## Acceptance checks

- Both audiences recognise their destination immediately on desktop and mobile.
- Both division actions have equal prominence and work with keyboard/touch.
- Real products replace illustrative hardware before publication.
- No unsupported credentials, tolerances, warranties or affiliations.
- Images reserve space and content stays usable while media loads or fails.
- Enquiries have genuine success/failure handling; no fake submission success.

## Next deliverable

Responsive homepage prototype, followed by one capability page, one product detail page and two appropriate enquiry paths. Validate the first viewport against the concept before expanding.
