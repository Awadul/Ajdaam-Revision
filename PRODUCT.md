# Ajdaam

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Present Ajdaam Machine Craft's engineering/manufacturing services and bath/sanitary products, helping visitors find relevant capabilities or products and make an enquiry. The user requires equal prominence for both divisions.

## Users

Confirmed audiences: B2B engineering/manufacturing buyers and sanitary-product buyers. Proposed audience segments to validate: procurement teams, architects, interior designers, contractors, dealers and project buyers. Their relative importance is not yet confirmed.

## Operating Context

Engineering visitors evaluate capabilities and discuss manufacturing requirements. Sanitary visitors explore products and request product/project information. The sanitary range is a mixture of products manufactured by Ajdaam and products supplied/distributed by it (explicitly confirmed by the user).

## Capabilities and Constraints

User-listed services: engineering, CNC machining, product design and manufacturing, metal casting and sand casting. The original list also says 'dyi casting'; do not silently publish this as die casting without clarification.

Existing project: D:/Ajdaam-Website/ajdaam-website. Inspection on 2026-10-25 found a Next.js 16.4.3 / React 19.3.0 / TypeScript / Tailwind 4 scaffold. Its app/page.tsx is still the default starter. No Ajdaam UI has been implemented by this conversation.

## Brand Commitments

Use Ajdaam as the working written name. Preserve the supplied black calligraphy, saffron field and red strokes. Original vector/high-resolution logo and final spelling/lockup need verification. User approved the warm industrial/luxury direction and requested equal prominence for both divisions.

## Evidence on Hand

Local reference copies are listed in CONTINUE.md. Homepage concept and mood board are AI-generated visual research, not evidence of actual products, equipment or facilities. Supplied reference screenshots include Marley, Rolls-Royce, DMG MORI and Dornbracht.

No verified product inventory, technical capacities, tolerances, machinery list, certificates, customer endorsements, distribution authorisations, prices, lead times, warranty terms or contact destination have been supplied. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Equal access to both divisions.
- Distinguish manufactured products from supplied products at item level; identify the manufacturer where verified.
- Do not imply authorised distribution without evidence.
- Keep product selection and technical evaluation practical.
- Make enquiries possible without a compulsory account or introductory tour.
