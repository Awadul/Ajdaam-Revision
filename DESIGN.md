## Do's and Don'ts

**The Evidence Rule.** Publish real product and process evidence; never present generated imagery as a catalogue or facility record.

**The Equal Access Rule.** Neither division becomes a secondary link or disappears behind the other's introduction.

**The Stillness Rule.** No floating products, breathing logos, pulsing buttons, automatic carousels or scroll hijacking. Do not delay navigation or reading for animation.

Use neutral catalogue lighting alongside atmospheric photography. Avoid theatrical machining sparks, vague prestige claims and invented credentials. Keep type and contrast readable; verify the real UI before calling it polished.
