# First-visit and enquiry experience

Status: proposed website flow, not implemented or user-tested. This is visitor onboarding, not an account setup process. No forced tour, splash screen, compulsory registration or remembered division that hides the other division.

## First value

Visitors understand Ajdaam's two divisions, select the relevant one and reach useful evidence. Engineering visitors should find a relevant capability; sanitary visitors should find an identifiable product and its provenance. These are design goals, not measured outcomes.

## Engineering route

Homepage or direct landing -> capability -> verified process/material/inspection evidence -> manufacturing enquiry. Keep navigation between capabilities available. Explain specialist terminology briefly when needed.

Proposed enquiry information: name, contact method and requirement summary; optional company, material, quantity and target date. Drawing uploads remain pending file-type, size, storage, retention and security decisions. Preserve typed data after validation or network failure. Do not promise reply times until agreed.

## Sanitary route

Homepage or direct landing -> actual category/collection -> product -> product/project enquiry. A visitor can explore independently or request selection help. Identify dimensions, finishes, manufacturer and manufactured/supplied status where verified. Include installation downloads only when real files exist.

Proposed enquiry information: name, contact method, product/reference and request; optional quantity or project context. Carry the selected product into the enquiry visibly and allow correction. Never imply a stock level or delivery date that has not been verified.

## States to design

- No search/filter results: explain and provide Clear filters plus product enquiry.
- Missing specification: say it is unavailable and offer Request details; do not invent a download.
- Failed product load: retry and an accessible enquiry route.
- Invalid form: field-specific explanation, error summary, preserved values and visible focus.
- Sending: prevent duplicates while showing progress.
- Failure: retain information and offer retry; contact alternative only if verified.
- Success: show acknowledgement only after actual acceptance; reference number only if supplied by the system.

## Accessibility and privacy

Use visible labels, understandable field requirements, keyboard operation, logical focus, adequate touch targets and screen-reader announcements. Do not rely on colour alone. Collect only necessary information; agree privacy notice, consent needs and retention before launch. Do not store sensitive enquiry/drawing contents in browser storage by default.

## Validation plan

Test both direct landing and homepage journeys. Check whether visitors can identify the right division and reach a relevant item without explanation. Test keyboard, touch, narrow screens, reduced motion, slow loading, interrupted forms and failed submissions. Analytics and success thresholds remain a launch decision; do not invent current conversion rates.
